How we began

Our Story

North Star Bakery began as a small family bread table at the neighborhood market. Local customers asked us to bake more, so we opened a bakery where people could meet, share food, and feel welcome.

Today, we still mix our dough in small batches and prepare each item with care. We are proud to serve commuters in the morning, families on the weekend, and event planners throughout the year.

Close to home

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We choose simple ingredients and buy from nearby farms when possible. We use local eggs, seasonal fruit, and flour from trusted regional mills. This helps us support other small businesses and serve fresh food to our community.
